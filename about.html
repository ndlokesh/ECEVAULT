Open Educational Resource

Empowering Every ECE Student

ECEVault is an open, student-driven academic portal built to democratize access to engineering education. We assemble verified lecture notes, university previous papers, laboratory manuals, and syllabus guides into one organized, intuitive repository.

💡 Why ECEVault

Our Guiding Principles

Designed specifically to eliminate academic barriers for undergraduate engineering students.

100% Free & Open

No registration, paywalls, or gated forms. Every document is immediately readable and downloadable with a single click.

Semester-Wise Clarity

Curated across Semester 1 through Semester 8 according to accredited university curriculum standards and verified course codes.

Quality Verified

All uploaded materials undergo review to ensure high typographical legibility, accurate diagrams, and aligned unit syllabi.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Here are the most common questions from students and contributors.

Do I need to sign up or create an account to view or download materials?

No. ECEVault is completely free and public. You never need to register, log in, or provide any personal details.

Can I preview PDFs directly in the browser?

Yes! Click the "Preview" button on any material card to open our lightweight document viewer equipped with page controls, zoom, and print functions.

Are the materials matched to standard university syllabi?

Yes, our subject categorizations, course codes, and unit outlines mirror standard AICTE and state university curricula for Electronics and Communication Engineering.

How can I contribute study materials or report an error?

You can contact our volunteer editorial team via the contact form or email below. We welcome handwritten notes, exam solutions, and lab records!
